The Platform

Everything your learning journey needs — in one place.

From live learning and content to assessments, communication, payments, approvals and analytics, Mentora Loop connects the full experience.

🎥

Live Classes

Scheduled live sessions with Google Meet integration, Join Now visibility and session history.

▶

Recorded Lectures

Teachers can upload real video lessons so learners can revisit content whenever they need it.

📚

Notes & Resources

Typed notes and file-based resources organized with the course and approved before publishing.

◉

Test Bank & Practice

Create MCQ tests, use unique access codes, import questions from Excel and run timed practice.

✓

Results & Gradebook

Auto-grade online MCQs or bulk-import offline results, publish marks and let learners ask questions.

↗

Excel Import & Export

Move test questions and paper-exam results in bulk, then export clean result data when needed.

💬

Community, Messages & Notifications

Course groups, direct messaging, announcements and a notification center keep mentors and learners connected without leaving the platform.

🗓

Calendar & Schedules

Real calendar views for classes and tests, recurring weekly schedules and upcoming-session reminders.

🔐

Admin Approval & Privacy

Accounts and content can stay locked until approved; sensitive details remain private and away from public profiles.

💳

Offline Fee Tracking

No online gateway is required. Admin records student fees and mentor payouts, with payment history visible to the right people.

📊

Analytics & Progress

Admin sees platform-wide trends; mentors see class performance; learners see their own score and progress patterns.

＋

Demand-Driven Courses

Learners can request a new topic. Popular requests become open vacancies that mentors can apply to teach.
