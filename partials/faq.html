Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Learn how MentoraLoop helps learners and mentors grow together, from exam preparation to home-based earning.

Who can learn or teach on MentoraLoop?

Anyone! Students preparing for MDCAT, ECAT, SAT, or university entrance exams, as well as anyone looking to learn or teach practical crafts like baking, crochet, tailoring, resin art, videography, or coding. Homemakers and skilled creators can easily launch classes and earn from home.

How does the 'Request a Class' feature work?

If you need preparation or guidance for a topic not currently listed, submit a request. When multiple learners show interest, MentoraLoop publishes an open vacancy, matches a verified mentor, and launches a dedicated live batch.

How do mentors and teachers get paid?

Mentors receive transparent, tiered payouts based on the number of enrolled learners in their batch. Payouts are transferred directly via Bank Transfer, Easypaisa, or JazzCash.

How do exams and assessments work?

MentoraLoop features an authentic Computer-Based Testing (CBT) simulator with countdown timers, an interactive question navigator, and instant scoring—mirroring real standardized entrance test software.

Is MentoraLoop safe for female educators and students?

Yes. MentoraLoop enforces strict admin moderation, verified mentor profiles, and controlled direct communication to guarantee a safe, respectful, and professional environment.
