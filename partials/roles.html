One central control layer

Admin keeps the whole ecosystem accountable.

Because Mentora Loop can operate with offline fee collection, admin acts as the central authority for approvals, access, content, classes, payments and platform-wide visibility.

  1. 1
    Approve peopleReview mentor and learner registrations, documents and account details.
  2. 2
    Control accessAssign classes, approve teaching tools and decide which learners can access which tests or courses.
  3. 3
    Moderate contentApprove lectures, notes, MCQs and tests before they become available where required.
  4. 4
    Manage money offlineRecord student fee payments, mentor payouts and payment notices without a gateway.
  5. 5
    See the whole platformMonitor classes, sessions, results, messages, analytics, schedules and activity history.
